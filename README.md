# BudgetTracker

A comprehensive budget tracking application designed to help you manage your finances efficiently and make informed financial decisions.

## Overview

BudgetTracker is a user-friendly application that enables individuals and families to track their income, expenses, and savings goals. With an intuitive interface and powerful features, you can gain complete visibility into your financial situation and optimize your spending habits.

## Features

- **Income & Expense Tracking**: Record and categorize all your financial transactions
- **Budget Management**: Set monthly budgets and monitor your spending against targets
- **Financial Reports**: Generate detailed reports and visualizations of your spending patterns
- **Category Management**: Organize transactions with customizable expense categories
- **Savings Goals**: Set and track progress toward your financial goals
- **Transaction History**: View complete history of all your transactions with filters and search
- **Alerts & Notifications**: Get notified when you're approaching budget limits
- **Data Export**: Export your financial data for analysis or backup purposes
